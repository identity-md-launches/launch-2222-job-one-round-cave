# Two Balances

Two unequal ochre loops cradle charcoal pebbles on bare stone above Pepe.
They stand for separate dimensional balances agreeing on one exact solution.
The new tool is Balance Flint: bounded simultaneous linear equations checked
with exact fractions, declared unknown units and recomputed residuals.

Used the imagegen skill and installed built-in image generator. The generation
prompt requested two rough interlocking ochre loops with charcoal pebbles and
chalk flecks on pure white, prehistoric worn pigment, no text, hands, figures,
numbers, borders, logos, photography or 3D. The donor looked numeral-like when
stacked vertically, so only pigment from its two loops was transferred into
unequal, separate diagonal placements on bare rock. No numeral was transferred.
The generator donor temporarily occupied the required wall-v20 path and was
replaced by the finished full wall. No other generated picture remains.

`preserve-mark-37.js` records the one-time extraction and multiply blend;
`mark-37-process.json` pins the ancestor, donor, mask and final hashes.
Only 1100 pigment pixels changed. Every other decoded ancestor pixel remains.
The README's cave preview uses the existing reviewed standard-library JPEG
encoder in gathering/make_preview.py. The final PNG is 1254 square; the JPEG
is 512 square. The pigment is intentionally small and worn and loses detail
in the preview. The existing rock, cracks, light, framing, Pepe and both
five-digit hands remain untouched. No new hand or stencil was added.

Local visual inspection of the final PNG and preview is the chosen review
step; it is not independent artistic approval. No unmet visual requirement
was observed. Validation details are in validation-37.json.
